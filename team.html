Meet the team

The people behind the service.

Families place a great deal of trust in the people supporting them. We think it matters to show who is behind the service and the values that shape the way we work.

Office team

Experienced leadership, recruitment, and coordination.

TH

Tori

Founder and Director

Tori is the Founder and Director of Time Specialist Support. Her background is in Speech and Language Therapy, and she has built the service around thoughtful matching, autism-specific understanding, and support that feels genuinely reliable for families.

AL

Ally

Service Manager

Ally joined Time in July 2016 and leads the day-to-day service as Service Manager. She supports families and staff across a wide range of situations, with a strong focus on consistency, communication, and giving people a positive experience of the service.

JA

Jack

Recruitment Co-ordinator

Jack is the Recruitment Co-ordinator at Time Specialist Support. He focuses on strengthening the support worker team and bringing in people who are well suited to autism-specific, relationship-led support work.

RI

Richard

Office Team

Richard has been part of Time for over 10 years. After starting as a support worker alongside teaching assistant work, he moved into the office team full-time and brings valuable frontline understanding to service coordination.

Why we show the team

The people behind the service matter.

Families often want more than a service description. They want to know who is making decisions, who is coordinating support, and what kind of people sit behind the organisation.

We think that visibility matters, because good support is built on trust as much as it is on process.

Wider support team

A broader team chosen for warmth, patience, reliability, and readiness to learn.

Carefully selected support workers

The live service highlights dependable, warm support workers who can build trust at the right pace and adapt well to autistic young people and family routines.

Student and early-career talent

Many of our workers are building experience in education, psychology, therapy, care, and related fields while developing strong practical skills.

Relationship-led support

Beyond qualifications, what matters most is patience, good judgment, and the ability to build positive, respectful relationships.
