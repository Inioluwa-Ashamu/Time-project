Meet the team

The people behind the service.

Families place a great deal of trust in the people supporting them. We think it matters to show who is behind the service and the values that shape the way we work.

Office team

Experienced leadership, recruitment, and coordination.

TH

Tori

Founder and Director

Tori brings a Speech and Language Therapy background and a long-standing commitment to autism-specific support. She leads the service with a strong focus on thoughtful matching, clear standards, and relationships that feel respectful and steady.

AL

Ally

Service Manager

Ally helps hold the day-to-day service together, supporting families and staff while keeping a close eye on quality, communication, and consistency.

JA

Jack

Recruitment Co-ordinator

Jack focuses on building a strong support worker team, helping Time recruit people who are capable, kind, and well suited to the work.

RI

Richard

Office Team

Richard’s experience links frontline support with the practical demands of office coordination, helping the service run smoothly behind the scenes.

Wider support team

A broader team chosen for warmth, patience, reliability, and readiness to learn.

Carefully selected support workers

We look for people who are dependable, warm, and able to build trust at a pace that feels right for the young person they are supporting.

Student and early-career talent

Many of our workers are building experience in education, psychology, therapy, care, and related fields while developing strong practical skills.

Relationship-led support

Beyond qualifications, what matters most is patience, good judgment, and the ability to build positive, respectful relationships.
