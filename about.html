About us

Built around trust, communication, and autism-specific understanding.

Time Specialist Support provides carefully matched support for autistic children, young people, and some adults, helping families feel confident that support is thoughtful, respectful, safe, and well organised.

Our purpose

Support that helps everyday life feel more manageable.

Every child, young person, and family is different. Routines, sensory needs, confidence levels, communication, and practical pressures all shape what helpful support looks like.

When families get in touch, we take time to understand the person and family context before matching support.

A note from Tori

Support works best when it feels personal, prepared, and steady.

Founder and Director

Time Specialist Support grew from a belief that autistic children and young people deserve support that is well matched and genuinely interested in who they are.

Families often come to us when day-to-day life feels stretched. Feeling listened to, knowing who to contact, and having confidence in the match all matter.

We want support to feel dependable in the details: communication, preparation, boundaries, and follow-through.

Standards

The things we pay attention to.

Matching well

Thinking carefully about fit, interests, availability, personality, and support needs.

Preparing properly

Making sure staff understand the person, family context, and expectations before starting.

Safeguarding seriously

Keeping safer recruitment, boundaries, reporting routes, and family trust at the centre.

Communicating steadily

Keeping expectations, updates, feedback, and review points easy to follow.

Values

What shapes the work.

Respect

Support that preserves dignity, voice, preference, and individuality.

Clarity

Families and referrers know what support looks like and why.

Consistency

Confidence grows when support is reliable, prepared, and coordinated.

Warmth

Support is calm, approachable, human, and professionally grounded.
