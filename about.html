About us

Built around trust, communication, and autism-specific understanding.

Time Specialist Support provides carefully matched, autism-specific support for children, young people, and some adults, helping families feel confident that support is thoughtful, respectful, and well organised.

Our purpose

Support that helps everyday life feel more manageable.

We know that every child, young person, and family is different. Routines, sensory needs, confidence levels, and practical pressures all shape what helpful support looks like.

When families get in touch, we take time to understand the person’s needs and the family context properly before matching support.

A note from Time

Support works best when it feels personal, prepared, and steady.

Families often come to us at points where day-to-day life feels stretched. In those moments, small things matter: feeling listened to, knowing who to contact, and having confidence that support has been matched with care.

We want the service to feel dependable from the outset, not just in principle but in the details of how we communicate, prepare, and follow through.

Person-led support

We adapt around communication style, sensory needs, interests, and routine rather than expecting a young person to adapt to a rigid service model.

Families matter too

Good support should reduce stress for the whole family, whether a family needs a regular break, occasional weekend support, or extra help during the holidays.

Quality through preparation

Matching, training, DBS checks, and trial sessions all contribute to safer, more confident, and more sustainable support.

Standards

The things we pay attention to.

Matching well

Thinking carefully about fit, not just filling availability.

Communicating clearly

Keeping expectations, updates, and next steps easy to follow.

Preparing properly

Making sure staff understand the person, family context, and expectations before starting.

Reviewing honestly

Being open about what is working, what is changing, and what needs more thought.

How we think

Values that shape the work.

Respect

We support people in ways that preserve dignity, voice, and individuality.

Clarity

Families and referrers should always understand what support looks like and why.

Consistency

Confidence grows when support is reliable, prepared, and professionally coordinated.

Warmth

Professionalism matters, but support should still feel human, approachable, and calm.
