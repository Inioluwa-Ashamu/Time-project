About us

Built around trust, communication, and autism-specific understanding.

Time Specialist Support exists to provide carefully matched, practical support for autistic children and young people while giving families confidence that care is thoughtful, respectful, and well organised.

Our purpose

Support that helps everyday life feel more manageable.

On the current website, Time Specialist Support describes itself as a provider of quality bespoke support for autistic young people and their families. This redesigned version develops that idea into a clearer public-facing narrative.

We are not trying to be generic. Our role is to deliver autism-specific support that pays attention to how the young person experiences the world, how the family is coping, and what consistency looks like in practice.

Person-led support

We adapt around communication style, sensory needs, interests, and routine rather than expecting a young person to adapt to a rigid service model.

Families matter too

Good support should reduce stress for the whole family. Clear communication and dependable staffing are a core part of that.

Quality through preparation

Matching, training, safeguarding expectations, and shadowing all contribute to safer, more confident, more sustainable support.

How we think

Values that shape the work.

Respect

We support people in ways that preserve dignity, voice, and individuality.

Clarity

Families and referrers should always understand what support looks like and why.

Consistency

Confidence grows when support is reliable, prepared, and professionally coordinated.

Warmth

Professionalism matters, but support should still feel human, approachable, and calm.
