Service areas

Autism support across Manchester, Salford, and Stockport.

Time Specialist Support works across Greater Manchester, with community-based support that is planned around each young person's routine, interests, communication, and confidence.

Local support

Support should feel practical in the places families actually live.

Matching is not only about personality. Location, travel, local activities, routine, and availability all affect whether support can work well week after week.

Autism support in Manchester

Community access, confidence-building, and interest-led sessions for families in Manchester and surrounding neighbourhoods.

Autism support in Salford

Local support coordinated from the office in Salford, with clear contact routes for families and professionals.

Autism support in Stockport

Support for young people and families in Stockport, including community access and activities planned around individual needs.

South Manchester sessions

Many support-worker opportunities and family sessions take place around South Manchester, depending on current matching and availability.

What local support can include

Confidence, access, and better routines in real settings.

Support may involve familiar routes, leisure activities, community spaces, specialist activities, travel preparation, and gradual steps into new experiences.

Community access

Helping young people access places and activities with preparation and support.

Family breathing space

Reliable sessions can reduce some of the pressure around planning and routine.

Careful matching

Matching considers the person, the worker, and the practical realities around them.
