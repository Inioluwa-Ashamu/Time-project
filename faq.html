Support questions

What families often want to know.

Do you provide autism support in Manchester?

Yes. Time Specialist Support works with families across Greater Manchester, including Manchester, Salford, Stockport, and nearby areas where matching and availability make support possible.

Do you offer respite care for autistic children?

Many families use our community-based support as a form of respite. The focus is still the child or young person having a positive, well-matched session, while parents and carers gain reliable breathing room.

How do you match a support worker?

We consider interests, personality, communication style, sensory needs, routine, location, availability, and any relevant preferences. The aim is a relationship that can build trust steadily.

What can support sessions include?

Sessions may include community access, familiar outings, specialist activities, shared interests, confidence building, and gradual support with new experiences. The plan depends on the young person and family priorities.

How do you approach safeguarding?

Safeguarding sits within recruitment, training, preparation, reporting routes, and day-to-day communication. Support workers are enhanced DBS checked and prepared before working independently.

Can professionals make referrals?

Yes. Professionals can contact us to discuss a possible referral, understand how the service works, or clarify what information would help us consider support.

What should I include in my first message?

A short outline is enough: who the enquiry is for, the kind of support needed, your area, any important routines or communication needs, and the best way to contact you.

What happens after I contact you?

The team reviews your enquiry, routes it to the right person, and agrees the next step with you. That might be a phone call, further information, signposting, or a discussion about matching.

Still unsure?

You do not need to have everything worked out before contacting us.

If you know support might help but are not sure what to ask for, send a short message. We can help you understand the next sensible step.
