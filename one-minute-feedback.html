Feedback

What to complete after sessions.

After every session, you will need to fill in a short feedback form that should only take around one minute to complete. You can also look this information up about any service user before your session with them by checking the sheet.

There is a short how-to video to explain each part of this system. Each video is about a minute long, so you should be able to watch any that are relevant to you in less than five minutes. Passwords for the videos that need one are all the same: tss.
