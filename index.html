Specialist support

Carefully matched support for autistic children, young people, and their families.

We provide autism-specific support that is thoughtful, dependable, and built around real family life. Our focus is on matching the right people, building trust, and helping young people feel more confident in the community.

What families come to us for

Support that feels calm, practical, and reliable.

  • Community access and befriending support
  • Autism-informed workers with enhanced DBS checks
  • Visual resources and social stories where helpful
  • Clear communication with families and referrers

Why families choose Time

Good support is often about the small things being done well.

Showing up on time. Matching people carefully. Explaining things clearly. Not rushing trust. Thinking ahead. Those things matter, and they shape the way we run the service.

Thoughtful matching

We look at interests, routine, personality, confidence, and practical fit.

Prepared support workers

Staff are trained, DBS checked, and properly prepared before working alone.

Clear communication

Families and professionals know who to contact and what to expect next.
