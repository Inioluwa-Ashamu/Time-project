Specialist support for autistic children, giving families confidence, structure, and peace of mind.

How it works

From first conversation to support that feels carefully matched.

1

Tell us what would help

We ask about family priorities, interests, communication, sensory needs, routine, timing, and location.

2

We look for the right match

3

Support starts carefully

Why families choose Time

Parents are trusting someone with their child, not just booking hours of support.

The details matter: who turns up, how prepared they are, whether communication is steady, and whether the support worker understands the young person as an individual.

Safer recruitment Enhanced DBS checks, references, orientation, and ongoing worker guidance.
Autism-informed planning Support planned around communication, sensory needs, routines, and interests.
Real team presence Families can see the people behind matching, recruitment, and coordination.
Local understanding Community-based support across Manchester, Salford, Stockport, and nearby areas.

In practice

Sessions are planned around real people, not a fixed script.

Support might be quiet and familiar, active and confidence-building, or focused on trying something new at the young person's pace.

Before Understand interests, routines, communication, sensory needs, and family priorities.
During Support community access, respite, shared activities, confidence, independence, and enjoyment.
After Keep families informed and notice what is working, changing, or worth trying next.
