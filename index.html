About Time Specialist Support

Human support, organised properly.

Thoughtful matching

We look at personality, interests, routine, practical needs, and availability to build support that feels natural rather than forced.

Confidence through consistency

Families need reliable people and reliable communication. We focus on both, so support is stable and easier to trust.

Better access to community life

From routine outings to specialist activities, support is designed to help young people participate in ways that feel safe and achievable.

What we do

A clearer support journey from first enquiry to day-to-day delivery.

01

Understand the situation

We start by listening carefully to the young person, family, and wider context so support is rooted in real needs rather than assumptions.

02

Match the right worker

Compatibility matters. We consider personality, interests, confidence, and practical requirements before a placement begins.

03

Prepare properly

Workers are supported with autism-specific training, safeguarding expectations, and trial sessions alongside experienced colleagues.

04

Review and adapt

Good support evolves. We stay responsive to feedback, changes in confidence, and changes in family circumstances.
