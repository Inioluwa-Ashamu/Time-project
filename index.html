About us

Support that feels steady, respectful, and well organised.

Time Specialist Support exists to reduce pressure for people navigating sensitive and often fast-moving circumstances. We create structured, human-centred support that protects dignity while helping everyone involved move forward with confidence.

Our approach balances empathy with accountability. That means careful listening, clear communication, and practical service coordination that can stand up in everyday life.

What we do

Specialist services built around continuity of care.

Initial guidance and referrals

We help people understand available options, clarify priorities, and identify the right route into support.

Care and support planning

We shape plans that are realistic, person-led, and easy for families and professionals to follow.

Case coordination

Our team keeps communication aligned across services so updates, actions, and responsibilities stay clear.

Family liaison

We provide a reassuring point of contact, helping families feel informed, involved, and supported.

Review and escalation support

As needs change, we reassess plans, surface risks early, and support timely next steps.

Professional partnership working

We collaborate with external agencies and services to create smoother, more consistent outcomes.

Meet the team
