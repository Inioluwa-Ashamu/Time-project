Work for us

Support work that is flexible, meaningful, and professionally grounded.

We are looking for people who are dependable, thoughtful, and genuinely interested in supporting autistic children and young people well, especially after school and at weekends.

Current opportunity

Support workers across Manchester, Salford, and Stockport, with many sessions in South Manchester.

What the role involves

Building supportive relationships, accompanying young people into the community, and delivering sessions that are typically 2 to 3 hours long.

Who the role suits

People who are motivated, well organised, and available after school and/or some weekends.

Why candidates apply

Flexible working, meaningful experience, and competitive pay currently advertised at £12.75 to £15 per hour.

What good support work looks like here

Warmth, consistency, and good judgment.

The role suits people who notice the small details, are comfortable building trust gradually, and understand that reliability matters just as much as enthusiasm.

Some applicants come with direct support experience. Others bring the right mindset and want to learn well. Both can work, if the person is grounded and genuinely thoughtful.

What Time offers

A role people can grow in.

Support work at Time suits people who want flexible, meaningful work and who are ready to learn from real relationships and real responsibility.

Flexible working

Useful for students and early-career professionals balancing study or other commitments.

Training and support

Structured onboarding and opportunities to strengthen your autism-specific practice.

Meaningful experience

Practical, relationship-based work that builds confidence, judgment, and real-world experience.

Equal opportunities

Fair recruitment matters.

We are committed to fair recruitment and to creating a workplace where people are treated with respect. If you need any adjustments during the recruitment process, we want you to feel comfortable asking.

Inclusive by design

Candidates should feel able to ask for reasonable adjustments during recruitment. Clarity, accessibility, and fairness are part of a professional hiring process.

Candidate voice

What people value about working at Time.

Flexible hours make the role easier to balance with university or other commitments.

The work helps build confidence, communication skills, and real-world professional maturity.

Relationships with young people are rewarding and often the most memorable part of the job.

Recruitment process

What to expect.

Initial contact

A first conversation to understand your interest, availability, and background, or answer questions at recruitment@time-specialist-support.com.

Safer recruitment checks

References, DBS requirements, and the usual checks needed for this kind of work.

Preparation and onboarding

Training, induction, and support to help you feel ready, with training sessions typically happening each month.

Ongoing support

Guidance from the team as you build experience and settle into the role.
