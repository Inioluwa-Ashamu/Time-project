Work for us

Flexible, meaningful support work with training and guidance.

We are looking for dependable, thoughtful people who are genuinely interested in supporting autistic children and young people well, especially after school and at weekends.

Current opportunity

Support workers across Manchester, Salford, and Stockport.

What the role involves

Building supportive relationships, accompanying young people into the community, and delivering sessions that are typically 2 to 3 hours long.

Who the role suits

People who are motivated, organised, warm, and available after school or at weekends.

Why candidates apply

Flexible working, meaningful experience, and competitive pay currently advertised at £13.45 to £15 per hour.
