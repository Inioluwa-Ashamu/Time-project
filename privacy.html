Privacy notice

How enquiry information is used.

This notice explains how Time Specialist Support handles information sent through the website, including support enquiries, recruitment questions, feedback, and complaints.

Website enquiries

Only share what is needed for the team to respond.

What we collect

The contact form asks for your name, email address, optional phone number, enquiry type, preferred contact route, message, and consent to respond.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond, route your message to the right person, discuss support or recruitment, handle feedback, and keep appropriate records.

What not to send

Please avoid sending unnecessary medical, diagnostic, financial, or highly sensitive personal detail through the website form.

Form processing

Website form submissions are sent to Time Specialist Support by FormSubmit. Email, phone, and external form links are handled by the relevant service providers.

Access and questions

To ask about information you have sent, contact info@time-specialist-support.com or call 0161 879 7984.

Emergencies

Do not use the website for emergencies or urgent safeguarding concerns. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
