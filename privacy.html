Privacy notice

How enquiry information is used.

This notice explains how Time Specialist Support handles information sent through the website, including support enquiries, recruitment questions, feedback, and complaints.

Website enquiries

Only share what is needed for the team to respond.

What we collect

The contact form asks for your name, email address, optional phone number, enquiry type, preferred contact route, message, and consent to respond.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond, route your message to the right person, discuss support or recruitment, handle feedback, and keep appropriate records.

Lawful basis

We use enquiry information because you have asked us to respond, because it is necessary for legitimate service administration, and, where relevant, because we may need to take steps before entering into an agreement or employment process.

Retention

We keep enquiry records only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, manage appropriate follow-up, meet legal or safeguarding duties, and maintain necessary business records. Detailed retention is governed by our Data Retention Policy.

Sharing and processors

Website form submissions are sent to Time Specialist Support by FormSubmit. Email, phone, external forms, Dropbox files, and Google links are handled by the relevant service providers. We do not sell enquiry information.

Your rights

You can ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where those rights apply. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

What not to send

Please avoid sending unnecessary medical, diagnostic, financial, or highly sensitive personal detail through the website form.

Access and questions

To ask about information you have sent, contact info@time-specialist-support.com or call 0161 879 7984.

Emergencies

Do not use the website for emergencies or urgent safeguarding concerns. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
