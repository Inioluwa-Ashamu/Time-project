Privacy, safety and accessibility

Clear expectations before you contact us.

This page explains how website enquiries are handled, what to do in an emergency, and how to ask for help accessing information on the site.

Using the contact form

The contact form asks for the information needed to route and respond to an enquiry. Please only include details you are comfortable sharing through a website form.

How details are used

Details sent through the form are used to respond to the enquiry, contact the person who submitted it, and decide which member of the team should follow up.

External form provider

The form is handled by FormSubmit so messages can be sent to the team by email. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in a first message.

Emergencies

This form is not monitored for emergencies. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 or contact the relevant safeguarding service.

Accessibility

Tell us if you need information in another way.

We want the site to be easy to read, navigate, and use on mobile and desktop. If you cannot access something, or you need information in a different format, contact the office and we will try to help.

You can call 0161 879 7984 or email info@time-specialist-support.com.

Questions

Need to ask about privacy, access, or safety?

Use the contact page for general questions, feedback, complaints, and accessibility requests. If your message is urgent, phone the office instead of relying on the form.
